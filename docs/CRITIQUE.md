# Critique log

One critic scored the game against the web-designer rubric (13 lines, 0 to 5).

- **Round 1:** 2 of 13 lines at 4 or more. Main hits: the globe was nearly invisible, the reveal didn't draw its
  lines, the lobby read as a form instead of a pre-game show.
- **Round 2:** 9 of 13 at 4 or more. Left: markers showing through the back of the globe, the final globe too small
  to read ("a marble"), the final table colliding with the slate, the phone home screen crowded.
- **Round 3 fixes:** markers hidden on the back face, flat map for wide games, the final panel placed under the slate,
  phone strip and padding, the "on air" strip on home, the phone globe moved up. Declined: moving "Leave this game"
  under the sticky start bar, because it's visible when you scroll and the bar is sticky on purpose.
- **Matrix (2026-11-09):** Windows 125/150%, High Contrast, Linux, Android, Firefox, Safari, 320px, reduced motion and
  translation stress all pass. High Contrast failed once (buttons lost their shape), fixed with a transparent border.
